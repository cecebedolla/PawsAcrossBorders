About our mission

We believe travel can strengthen local dog rescue work.

Paws Across Borders was created for travelers who want to help, but also want to do it responsibly. We center the needs of local rescuers, veterinarians, foster families, and the dogs already in their care.

01

Local leadership first

We feature organizations that define their own needs and welcome travelers into clearly scoped support roles.

02

Animal welfare always

Listings prioritize safe handling, disease prevention, enrichment, and long-term outcomes over photo opportunities.

03

Transparent expectations

Travelers can compare time commitments, required skills, costs, housing options, and daily responsibilities before applying.

Our approach

From quick visits to long-term impact

Not every traveler can volunteer hands-on, and that is okay. A supply delivery, adoption flyer translation, remote grant support, or flight escort can be just as valuable when it matches what a rescue actually needs.

  • Screened partner profiles and practical trip notes
  • Responsible volunteer guidelines for every destination
  • Connections to donate, foster, transport, or advocate
